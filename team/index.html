Amisha Sethi

Senior marketing leader, award-winning actor, bestselling author and Mrs. India Worldwide 2021.

2021Mrs. India Worldwide

Amisha has spent more than 21 years in global marketing and leadership, at brands including BlackBerry, AirAsia, Airtel, Locus and Inflect Inc. She serves as Senior Vice President, Policy & Communication at the Financial Planning Standards Board (FPSB) India.

She is an executive scholar of the Kellogg School of Management, Northwestern University, and holds an MBA from Amity Business School. In 2012, Bloomberg and CMO Asia named her one of India's Top 50 Brand Leaders.

Outside the boardroom, she is an award-winning actor and a bestselling author. Her performance in the film Mehar won recognition for Best Actress at two festivals in 2024, and her earlier film Dronacharya streams on Disney+ Hotstar and MX Player. Her book, It Doesn't Hurt to Be Nice, blends humour, spirituality and self-discovery. A motivational speaker, certified yoga practitioner and dancer, she was crowned Mrs. India Worldwide 2021.

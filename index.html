Our story

Two minds. Two worlds. One story.

UNBOUND didn't start with a business plan. It started with two people who kept seeing the same problem from opposite sides of the table.

HimaniPath one
AmishaPath two
UNBOUNDWhere the paths meet
01 / 04
  1. 01 · Two paths

    Different industries. Different instincts.

    Himani and Amisha built their careers in different worlds, in different rooms, with different playbooks. One learned how a story earns its place in the headlines. The other learned how a brand earns its place in people's lives.

  2. 02 · The same frustration

    Every brand, five agencies, five stories.

    Wherever they worked, they watched the same thing happen. The PR agency told one story. The social team told another. The media agency bought space for a third, and the events crew improvised a fourth. Brands were paying for noise instead of a voice.

  3. 03 · The meeting point

    One question changed everything.

    When their paths crossed, the conversation was short: what if one team owned the whole story, from the press release to the billboard, from the mall activation to the launch night?

  4. 04 · UNBOUND

    So they built it.

    A whole marketing department that brands can bring in on day one: no walls between disciplines, no layers between you and the founders, and no playbook it has to follow. Two minds, one team, every channel. Your brand, unbound.

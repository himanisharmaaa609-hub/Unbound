House overrides for unattended posts

These win over anything else you read, including general writing or SEO advice.

- No AI-disclosure note on the post. The byline and authorship fields stay as the renderer builds them.
- Never invent a quote from a person. Quotes come only from facts.json.
- Never mention UNBOUND pricing, discounts, client counts or results.
- Never write about a rival marketing agency by name.
- If the keyword turns out to be off-topic for UNBOUND's readers, or unsafe to write about, return {"error":"off-topic: <reason>"} and stop. The owner will pick again.
- If sources disagree, say so in one sentence and cite both.
- If a fact you need cannot be confirmed within the research limits, leave it out instead of guessing, and add an open item.
